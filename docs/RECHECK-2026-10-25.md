## Confirmed failures and fixes

1. **Overlapping watch saves:** pausing a native-style storage acknowledgement, navigating away and opening a fresh Watch log allowed a second stale snapshot to replace the first. App-level synchronous and rendered write guards now keep navigation/modes and other mutations locked until acknowledgement. Failed writes retain the draft and saved data; retry adds one observation. Schema and saved IDs are unchanged.
2. **Small-screen diagram collapse:** at 360×640 the old diagram viewport reached zero height; at 915×412 lower actions overflowed the screen. The diagram page now scrolls with a bounded minimum working area and an explicitly measured height. All three panes, inspectors and essential actions remain reachable across the tested dimensions; nested zoom/pan is retained.
3. **Unknown-setting validation:** inherited object names such as `constructor` passed the configuration key check. Own-property validation rejects them before mutation, including when advancing an externally supplied state. Current fixed-field UI could not submit these names.
